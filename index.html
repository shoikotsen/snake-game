How to play

Arrow keys or WASD to steer, swipe on a phone. Space or P pauses, R restarts.
Mouse: +10 and grow. Golden mouse: +50, but it runs off after 8 seconds. Blue crystal: slows time for 6 seconds. The snake speeds up every 5 apples. In Hunt mode the mouse scurries away from you, and each catch is worth 15.
